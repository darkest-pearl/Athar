# Athar: The Three Fundamental Principles

English course and question draft 0.1. Prepared 30 October 2026.

This proposes a concept-based course with optional supporting audio. The 45 sections cover the book's main sequence; the 90 questions are starter pairs, not an exhaustive conversion of every discussion in the commentary. Every item is an unapproved authoring draft.

## How you control it

Use the workbook to mark each concept and each question **Keep, Edit, Remove, or Defer**. Leave it **Undecided** until you decide. Enter **Your order** to change the proposed sequence. You may keep an easy question and remove its review variant, or the reverse. Use stable IDs in your feedback. A Keep decision selects a candidate; religious and language reviews still apply to its exact version.

## The learning process

1. Show one short concept card and an original animated book companion.
2. Ask a supported three-choice question. Help the learner understand why the answer is right.
3. Explain mistakes immediately and let the learner try again.
4. Revisit the same concept later with a different prompt or four plausible choices. Remove the teaching card from view for an unassisted retention attempt.
5. Return to the concept in mixed and spaced practice. Start with a same-session revisit and adjustable reviews near 1, 3, 7, 14, and 30 days.
6. Show completed lessons, concepts needing review, and concepts recalled independently. An obvious first answer is practice, not proof of durable learning.

This schedule and the difficulty sequence are Athar proposals. Retrieval-with-feedback and spacing have experimental support, but the sources do not establish this exact schedule as optimal for every learner or for this course. Audio is optional; access to the underlying explanation remains available.
