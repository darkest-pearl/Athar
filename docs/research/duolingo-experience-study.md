# Bounded learner-experience study · 21 October 2026

This is pattern extraction and adaptation, not a mirror. The reference is Duolingo's public learner journey; Athar remains an original, offline, audio-led native Android app. Labels below separate visible/public evidence from design inference. No Duolingo private scheduler, scoring rule, or personalization was recovered.

## Method and access ceiling

Mimic [`a2e1f6c`](https://github.com/sumanrox/mimic/tree/a2e1f6c3316bf10b6e94e8898981d6fef768e670) was inspected in an ignored `.local/mimic` checkout: README, `skill/SKILL.md`, capture, motion and access-wall guides, and `measure.js`. Its E (pattern extraction) and F (adaptation) modes informed the state inventory and observe → specify → validate loop. The checkout is a study tool; no Mimic code was copied into Athar. The reviewed scripts expect a Claude/Playwright browser evaluation surface and DOM/CSS; the available computer-use browser inventory was empty, so `detect.js`, `measure.js`, motion sampling, asset copying and pixel verifier were **not run**. The checkout retains its [MIT license](https://github.com/sumanrox/mimic/blob/a2e1f6c3316bf10b6e94e8898981d6fef768e670/LICENSE). Node ≥18 and Playwright are declared for its web tooling; their Windows behavior is unverified here.

The live `duolingo.com/learn` response exposed no learner DOM to the text browser, and no interactive browser was available. Therefore no signed-in or live mobile-web exercise, actual mobile CSS dimensions, live transitions, Back/Exit behavior, or timing was observed. The capability ceiling is **source-documented public screenshots and explanations**, not live web fidelity. Official screenshots below are reference links, not Athar assets. They may depict iOS/Android examples; no Android-specific behavior is inferred unless the source says so. No login, credential, private session, or copyrighted asset was collected.

These become Athar requirements tested on Athar's emulator, not claims about Duolingo.

## Extraction ledger

Adopt: ordered authored units, visible current/completed/upcoming/review status, one short activity at a time, explicit correction, return to previous material, a truthful completion screen, and separate daily participation signals. Adapt: Duolingo's language-text exercises to Athar's reviewed audio passage → takeaway → source-linked question sequence; use the Garden palette and original components. Exclude: brand characters, XP, hearts, leagues, streak pressure, proprietary assets, and any inferred Duolingo scheduling or grading algorithm. No timing or pixel-fidelity claim is made from the public screenshots.
