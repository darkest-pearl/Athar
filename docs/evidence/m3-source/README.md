# Source-passage and timeline QA · ElmPrototype API 36

The QA course is `content/qa-passage-pack.json`, imported through Android's document picker. Its two small, deterministic non-speech WAVs are generated by `scripts/generate_audio_qa.py`; both have SHA-256 and byte counts in the pack. This course is a technical fixture, not teaching content. The 21 original MP3 files were not opened, changed, or committed during this check. Wi-Fi and mobile data were disabled before the document checks.

Observed on the emulator with the actual player UI:

- Cue A lesson clip: original 0:02–0:08, displayed 0:00–0:06. A paused drag moved its thumb/position to 0:02; a drag during playback retained the Pause control and advanced playback. Seeking alone left study progress at zero.
- Quiz question 1 feedback: wrong answer showed its explanation and **Neutral cue A**, original 0:06–0:08, displayed 0:00–0:02. The saved full-recording position of 0:08 on the 0:12 track did not carry into the passage. Opening full restored 0:08; returning to passage reset to 0:00.
- Quiz question 2 feedback selected **Neutral cue B**, original 0:02–0:04, with a separate 0:00–0:02 passage timeline. The lesson's cue A source was not reused.
- Selecting `a-440.wav` for cue A produced a checksum mismatch and preserved the previous working source. Selecting the matching cue A document succeeded offline. After deleting only that selected emulator document and reopening, the player showed unavailable audio, unknown duration, and disabled seeking; restoring/reselecting it succeeded offline without fallback.
- Leaving the app for Home after playback had advanced returned to a paused 0:03 lesson position. Background playback is not enabled. The emulator was run without audible-output assessment; matching titles, ranges, player positions, and generated tone frequencies do not substitute for human listening.
